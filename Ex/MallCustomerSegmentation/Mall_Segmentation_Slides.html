Mall Customer Segmentation

Unlocking Customer Value through Data

Method: K-Means Clustering & Silhouette Analysis

1. Introduction & Objective

The Mission: Move beyond generic marketing by identifying mathematically distinct customer personas.

  • Data: Annual Income ($k) vs Spending Score (1-100).
  • Challenge: Human intuition fails at scale. We need Unsupervised Learning.
  • Goal: Validate customer segments using K-Means and Silhouette Analysis.

2. Steps Followed

  1. Data Simulation: Generated synthetic data mimicking Kaggle's "Mall Customers" (Income/Score clusters).
  2. Preprocessing: Applied StandardScaler so Income ($15k-$140k) doesn't overpower Score (1-100).
  3. Analysis Loop: Tested K=2, 3, 4, 5. Calculated Inertia (tightness) and Silhouette (separation).
  4. Validation: Used Elbow Plot and Knife Plots to select Optimal K.

3. Detailed Output Analysis

  • The Elbow Plot: Shows a clear bend at K=5. Adding a 6th cluster yields diminishing returns.
  • Silhouette Score: Peaked at K=5 (>0.6).
  • Knife Plot: Showed 5 thick, even "blades". This confirms we don't have "weak" or "ambiguous" clusters.

Verdict: Validated 5 Distinct Market Segments.

4. Observations: The 5 Personas

  • Type 1: Sensible Savers: High Income, Low Spend. (Needs Value Prop)
  • Type 2: VIPs: High Income, High Spend. (The "Whales" - Retention Key)
  • Type 3: Impulse Crowd: Low Income, High Spend. (Trend sensitive)
  • Type 4: Budget Baseline: Low Income, Low Spend.
  • Type 5: Balanced Middle: Average on both. (Reliable Volume)

5. Strategic Recommendations

Strategy Shift: Move from "One-Size-Fits-All" to "Persona-Protocol".

  • Protocol A (VIPs): Elite loyalty programs, no discounts.
  • Protocol B (Savers): Focus on "Investment Quality" marketing.
  • Protocol C (Impulse): Flash sales & Instagram ads.

Conclusion: K=5 segmentation allows for precise targeting, maximizing ROI per email sent.
