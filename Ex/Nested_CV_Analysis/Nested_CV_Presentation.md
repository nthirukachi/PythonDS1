# Nested Cross-Validation Analysis
## Computation Estimates & Optimization

### Problem: Estimating Compute Budget for 5x4 Nested CV

---

# Problem Overview

**The Setup:**
*   **Dataset**: 12,000 samples
*   **Method**: Nested Cross-Validation
*   **Outer Loop**: 5 Folds
*   **Inner Loop**: 4 Folds (Grid Search)
*   **Hyperparameters**: 18 Combinations (Initial Plan)
*   **Costs**:
    *   Training: 7 min/model
    *   Refit: 10 min/model

**Objectives:**
1.  Compute Total Training Runs.
2.  Compute Total Training Time (Hours).
3.  Compare with reduced grid (10 combinations).

---

# Step 1: Breakdown the Workflow

To solve this, we separate the process into two distinct phases for cost calculation:

**Phase A: The Inner Grid Search**
*   This runs $K_{inner} \times N_{combos}$ times for *every* outer fold.
*   Goal: Find best hyperparameters.

**Phase B: The Outer Refit**
*   This runs 1 time for *every* outer fold.
*   Goal: Train final estimator for that fold.

---

# Step 2: Formulate the Math

We derived the following formulas:

$$
\text{Total Runs} = (\text{Outer} \times \text{Inner} \times \text{Combos}) + (\text{Outer} \times 1)
$$

$$
\text{Total Time} = (\text{Inner Runs} \times 7\text{min}) + (\text{Refit Runs} \times 10\text{min})
$$

Where:
*   $\text{Outer} = 5$
*   $\text{Inner} = 4$

# Scenario A: 18 Combinations

Let's plug in the numbers for the initial plan.

*   **Inner Runs**: $5 \times 4 \times 18 = 360$ runs.
*   **Refit Runs**: $5$ runs.
*   **Total Runs**: $365$.

*   **Time Calculation**:
    *   Inner: $360 \times 7 = 2520$ min.
    *   Refit: $5 \times 10 = 50$ min.
    *   Total: $2570$ min.

**Result**: **42.83 Hours**

---

# Scenario B: 10 Combinations

What if we reduce the grid to 10 parameters?

*   **Inner Runs**: $5 \times 4 \times 10 = 200$ runs.
*   **Refit Runs**: $5$ runs.
*   **Total Runs**: $205$.

*   **Time Calculation**:
    *   Inner: $200 \times 7 = 1400$ min.
    *   Refit: $50$ min.
    *   Total: $1450$ min.

**Result**: **24.17 Hours**

---

# Final Output & Comparison

| Metric | Scenario A (18) | Scenario B (10) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Runs** | 365 | 205 |
| **Total Time** | 42.83 hrs | 24.17 hrs |

### Observations
*   **Time Saved**: Reducing the grid size by ~44% (18 $\to$ 10) saved **43.58%** of the time.
*   **Dominant Factor**: The fixed overhead of "refitting" (50 mins) is negligible. The cost is almost purely driven by the inner grid search.
*   **Operational Impact**: This reduction shifts the workload from a "2-day job" to a "1-day job".

---

# Conclusion

*   Nested CV is **extremely expensive** ($O(K_{out} \times K_{in} \times N_{params})$).
*   Even small reductions in the hyperparameter grid have massive payoffs in compute time.
*   For the 18-combination case, budget for **~43 hours** of GPU time.
